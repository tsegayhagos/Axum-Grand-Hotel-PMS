# Module 07: Hospitality Reporting, KPIs & Business Analytics

## 1. Overview
Delivers executive visibility into hotel yields, revenue performance, operational bottlenecks, and financial reconciliation.

## 2. Core Hospitality KPI Formulas (`hotel.kpi.dashboard`)

### A. Occupancy Rate (%)
Measures the proportion of available inventory occupied by paying guests:
$$\text{Occupancy Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Occupied Rooms}}{\text{Total Salable Rooms}} \right) \times 100$$

### B. ADR (Average Daily Rate)
Measures the average rental revenue earned per occupied room:
$$\text{ADR} = \frac{\text{Total Room Revenue}}{\text{Total Number of Occupied Rooms}}$$

### C. RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)
The premier hospitality metric combining occupancy and pricing power:
$$\text{RevPAR} = \frac{\text{Total Room Revenue}}{\text{Total Salable Rooms}} = \text{Occupancy Rate} \times \text{ADR}$$

### D. Departmental Revenue Mix
Tracks revenue contribution by outlet:
- Rooms Department Revenue
- Food & Beverage / Minibar Revenue
- Laundry & Valet Services Revenue
- Miscellaneous & Valet Parking Revenue

## 3. Executive Dashboard & Manager Reports
- **Executive Real-time Dashboard:** Live cards showing Occupancy %, ADR, RevPAR, and Today's Gross Revenue.
- **Operations Snapshot:** Arrivals today, Departures today, In-house VIP count, Pending Housekeeping tasks, Open Maintenance tickets.
- **Daily Manager Flash Briefing:** Printable executive summary combining physical room rack distribution with trial balance financials.
- **QWeb PDF Printouts:**
  - Official Guest Folio Statement & Tax Bill.
  - Guest Registration Card for arrival signature.
